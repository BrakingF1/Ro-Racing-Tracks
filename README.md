# Ro-Racing Tracks
A respiratory with a list of Ro-Racing tracks (project model files) that are added for preservation reasons. They can be used for league racing, inspiration, and general game use. Not all or one of these files belong to me, credits to their respective authors/creators.

## ⚠️ Warning ⚠️
Remember if you get **<ins>IP violated<ins>** by publishing a model or game in Roblox, make your own racing tracks from scratch instead. This is the best way to get inspired by referring the tracks above, and you won't get sued for copyright infringement from verified users.

### Origins
On Spring 2018, I was able to preserve KiboyR tracks thanks to the giveaway that started a new wave of modernizing ro-racing tracks for leagues and other games using them inspired by what they saw, and that's how a couple sanctioned leagues like pre-ION Racing (faasta's league) and Alliance Stock Car Association (ASCA) started from the ground up. In Summer 2021 and first half of 2022, another wave of tracks was distributed for free, but with certain hardware limitations that prevented this to happen more often. Nowadays, I am on a mission to not let these tracks being abandoned and hope it runs well along the way. You can CTRL+F to find which tracks you are looking for.
